Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Paragon Central ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you fill out a form on this site (for a free consultation, a career application, or a general inquiry), we collect the information you provide, which may include your full name, mobile number, email address, and any details you share about your insurance or coaching interests.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, follow up about a consultation or job application, and provide the services you've requested. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Sharing

Your information may be shared with the licensed financial advisor or coach assigned to follow up with you. We also use Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising tools, so anonymized or aggregated data about visits to this site and actions taken on it (such as submitting a form) may be shared with Meta Platforms, Inc. to measure ad performance and show our ads to people more likely to be interested in them. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Retention & Security

We retain the information you submit only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or maintain our business records, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorized access.

Your Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Cookies & Advertising Technologies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure and improve the performance of our Facebook and Instagram ads. The Meta Pixel may collect information about your visit (such as pages viewed and forms submitted) and share it with Meta for these purposes. You can control how Meta uses this information for ads through your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, and you can block cookies generally through your browser settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
