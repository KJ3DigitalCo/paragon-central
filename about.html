Company History

A team built by professionals who chose this work

Paragon Central was built by professionals who chose this work deliberately, coming from diverse industries and united by a shared commitment to excellence in financial services. The team has grown through mentorship, education, and a standard that puts every client relationship first.

Paragon Central carries that standard forward. A team of coaches who treat every client relationship as a long-term commitment, not a single transaction.
